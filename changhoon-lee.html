About

A Structural Engineer at Heart

ChangHoon Lee is an Associate Professor in the Civil & Environmental Engineering department at Western New England University, where they have spent years doing what they love most: getting their hands on concrete and helping students understand why structures stand. Their expertise spans reinforced concrete design, structural mechanics, and the science of cement-based materials — from chemical and mechanical properties to multi-scale characterization and environmental durability.

Ask them what they do, and the answer is simple: they're a concrete guy. Not by accident — by conviction. There is something honest about concrete. It is heavy and slow, and when you get the mix right, it outlasts everything around it. Lee brings that same unhurried precision to the classroom, where the goal is always the same: build understanding that holds.

Teaching

Courses

CEE 341

Structural Analysis

A rigorous treatment of statically determinate and indeterminate structures — beams, trusses, frames — using classical and matrix methods. Emphasis on engineering judgment alongside analytical skill: knowing not just how a structure behaves, but why.

ENGR 103

Introduction to Engineering

A first-year survey that introduces students to the engineering profession, problem-solving methodology, and hands-on design. A course Lee finds as energizing to teach as any upper-division subject — the enthusiasm in the room is unmatched.

Spring Semester

ENGR 110

Data Acquisition & Processing

Hands-on introduction to sensor systems, data loggers, and signal processing — the instrumentation layer that sits between physical phenomena and the numbers engineers use. Connects directly to Dr. Lee's work in concrete stiffening measurement.

CEE 342

Steel & Reinforced Concrete Structures

Design of structural members in steel and reinforced concrete, following AISC and ACI 318. Covers beams, columns, connections, and composite systems — the practical extension of structural analysis into real construction.

CEE 451

Construction Materials

The properties, testing, and selection of materials used in construction: concrete, steel, wood, asphalt, and composites. Dr. Lee's home territory — from mix design to maturity prediction to durability assessment.

Research

Selected Publications

Dr. Lee's scholarly work centers on cement-based materials — their chemical and mechanical properties, multi-scale characterization, and how temperature, moisture, and aggregate morphology affect durability and structural performance. Recent work extends to bond-slip behavior in corrosion-damaged reinforced concrete.
